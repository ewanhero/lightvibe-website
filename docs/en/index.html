GET CONNECTED

Three devices. One flow.

  1. 01

    Prepare your Mac

    When the release is available, install LightVibe Bridge and follow its Codex compatibility check. Keep your Mac running and connected.

  2. 02

    Connect and pair

    Connect iPhone and Mac to the same trusted network, then scan the Bridge pairing code in LightVibe. Keep pairing codes private.

  3. 03

    Choose a task. Speak.

    Confirm your target task and grant the required microphone, speech recognition and notification permissions. Test alerts on each device.

The first release does not include approval alerts or mobile approval actions. Local pairing does not enable remote commands outside your network.
